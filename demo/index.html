Cypress Harbor Credit Union

A fictional credit union used to show the platform at business scale: 340 employees in 11 departments, 11 branches in South Florida, one on-prem GPU server, and a library of 59 documents: 52 for staff and 7 written for members.

Its people, documents and usage numbers are invented for this demo. Answer-quality results are measured on the real code.

Generated by scripts/sample_library.py and scripts/generate_sample_company.py from a fixed seed.
