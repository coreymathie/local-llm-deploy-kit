# Consumer Lending Guidelines

These guidelines apply to all consumer loan officers.

## Auto loans

New and used auto loans have a maximum term of 84 months. Used vehicles older than 10 model years are limited to 48 months. Loan-to-value may reach 125 percent of retail value for members with a credit score of 700 or higher.

## Debt-to-income

The standard debt-to-income limit is 43 percent. Loan officers may approve up to 48 percent with two compensating factors, such as 12 months of reserves or a stable two-year employment history.

## Exceptions

Exceptions beyond these limits go to the lending manager, who records the reason in the loan file before closing.
