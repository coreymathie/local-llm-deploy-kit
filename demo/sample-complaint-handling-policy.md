# Member Complaint Handling (Cypress Harbor Credit Union, fictional sample)

This is a fictional sample policy for demonstration purposes only.

## Logging

Every complaint is logged in the CRM on the day it is received, including complaints made in a branch, by phone, by email or on social media.

## Response times

Acknowledge the complaint within 2 business days. Resolve it, or explain what is still needed, within 15 business days.

## Escalation

Complaints that mention discrimination, a regulator, an attorney or elder financial abuse are escalated to the compliance department the same day. The compliance team reviews complaint trends with the board's audit committee every quarter.
