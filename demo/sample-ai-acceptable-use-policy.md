# Acceptable Use of AI Assistants (Cypress Harbor Credit Union, fictional sample)

This is a fictional sample policy for demonstration purposes only.

## Approved tools

Employees may use the internal AI assistant, which runs on credit union infrastructure and only answers from documents the employee is allowed to read. Public chatbots are blocked on credit union devices.

## Member data

Never paste member names, account numbers, card numbers or Social Security numbers into any AI tool outside the internal assistant.

## Reviewing answers

The assistant cites its sources. Check the cited document before acting on an answer that affects a member's money, a loan decision or a complaint. Report wrong or uncited answers to the AI governance committee.
