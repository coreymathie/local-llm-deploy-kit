# Branch Security Procedures

This procedure applies to every branch and its staff.

## Opening and closing

Two employees open and close every branch together. One enters while the other waits outside until the all-clear signal is given.

## Vault and cash

The vault operates under dual control and a time lock. Teller drawers are limited to $15,000; excess cash goes to the vault within the hour.

## Robbery response

Comply with demands, activate the silent alarm only when it is safe, and lock the doors after the suspect leaves. Do not discuss the event on social media; refer every media question to the marketing director.
