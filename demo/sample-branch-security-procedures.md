# Branch Security Procedures

This procedure applies to every branch and its staff.

## Opening and closing

Two employees open and close every branch together. One enters while the other waits outside until the all-clear signal is given.

## Vault

The vault operates under dual control and a time lock. Vault combinations are changed every 6 months and whenever an employee who knows one leaves the credit union.

## Alarms and cameras

Test the robbery alarm with the monitoring company on the first business day of each month. Camera recordings are kept for 90 days and released to law enforcement only through the facilities and security manager.
