# Data Retention and Records Policy

This policy applies to all employees and all credit union records.

## Retention periods

Financial records, including invoices and expense reports, are retained for seven years. Employee personnel files are retained for four years after the employee leaves. System audit logs are retained for six years and archived to write-once storage every month. Marketing contact lists are reviewed every twelve months and inactive contacts are deleted.

## Deletion requests

Requests from individuals to delete their personal data must be acknowledged within 5 business days and completed within 30 days, unless a legal hold applies. The privacy team records each request and its outcome.

## Legal holds

When litigation is reasonably anticipated, the legal team issues a legal hold. A legal hold suspends deletion for the affected records until the hold is released in writing.

## Storage locations

Records containing customer information must be stored in approved systems only. Personal cloud drives and personal email accounts are not approved storage locations.
