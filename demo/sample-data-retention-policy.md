# Data Retention and Records Policy

This policy applies to all employees and all credit union records.

## Retention periods

Most records are kept for seven years. Member account records and loan files are kept for seven years after the account is closed or the loan is paid off. Financial records, including invoices and expense reports, are retained for seven years. Employee personnel files are retained for four years after the employee leaves. System audit logs are retained for six years and archived to write-once storage every month. Marketing contact lists are reviewed every twelve months and inactive contacts are deleted.

## Disposal

Records that reach the end of their retention period are destroyed within 90 days by shredding or certified deletion. A member who closes an account cannot shorten the retention period, because regulatory record-keeping rules set it. The records team logs every disposal batch.

## Legal holds

When litigation is reasonably anticipated, the legal team issues a legal hold. A legal hold suspends disposal for the affected records until the hold is released in writing.

## Storage locations

Records containing member information must be stored in approved systems only. Personal cloud drives and personal email accounts are not approved storage locations.
