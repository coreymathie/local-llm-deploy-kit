# Wire Transfer Verification

This procedure applies to payments operations and branch staff.

## Callback verification

Every outgoing wire over $5,000 requires a callback to the phone number already on file. Never call a number supplied in the request itself, even if the member insists.

## Dual approval

Wires over $50,000 need a second approver from payments operations. The same employee cannot both enter and approve a wire.

## Cutoff and red flags

The domestic wire cutoff is 3:00 pm Eastern. Escalate to the fraud team before sending if the member was recently added as a joint owner, changed their email or phone in the last 7 days, or is sending funds to cryptocurrency exchanges.
