# BSA/AML Escalation Procedure

This procedure applies to the Compliance and BSA department and is administered by the BSA officer. Front-line employees refer unusual activity under the Unusual Activity Referral Policy.

## Reviewing referrals

A BSA analyst reviews every referral within 5 business days, checks the member's transaction history and related accounts, and opens a case file.

## Filing decisions

The BSA officer has 30 days from the referral to decide whether to file a suspicious activity report, and records the decision and the reasoning in the case file.

## Confirmed OFAC matches

A confirmed OFAC match is blocked or rejected and reported to OFAC within 10 business days.

## Confidentiality

Never tell a member that a referral or a filing was made, and do not note it in the member's CRM record.
