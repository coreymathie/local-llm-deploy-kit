# BSA/AML Escalation Procedure

This procedure applies to all employees and is administered by the BSA officer.

## Unusual activity referrals

Any employee who sees unusual activity submits a referral to the BSA team within 24 hours. Examples include cash deposits structured just under $10,000 and rapid movement of funds through a new account.

## Investigation

The BSA officer decides whether to file within 30 days of the referral and records the decision and the reasoning in the case file.

## Confidentiality

Never tell a member that a referral or a filing was made, and do not note it in the member's CRM record.
