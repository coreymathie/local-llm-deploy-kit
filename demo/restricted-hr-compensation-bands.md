# Compensation Bands (HR only, Cypress Harbor Credit Union, fictional sample)

This is a fictional sample document for the demo. It is visible only to callers in the hr group.

## Salary bands

Level 1 salary band is $52,000 to $64,000 per year. Level 2 salary band is $63,000 to $78,000 per year. Level 3 salary band is $77,000 to $95,000 per year. Level 4 salary band is $94,000 to $118,000 per year.

## Review cycle

Salary reviews happen once a year in March. Out-of-cycle adjustments need approval from the HR director and finance.

## Confidentiality

Band data must not be shared outside HR. Managers receive the band for their own reports only.
