# Remote Work Policy

This policy applies to all non-branch employees.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period may request to work remotely up to three days per week. Requests are approved by the employee's manager and recorded by People Operations.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, Monday through Friday.

## Equipment and stipend

The company provides a laptop and a monitor. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair, or headset. Receipts for the stipend must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Security requirements

Company data may only be accessed on company-managed devices. A VPN connection is required on any network that is not your home network, including hotels and coffee shops. Printed documents containing customer information must be shredded, not placed in household recycling. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.
