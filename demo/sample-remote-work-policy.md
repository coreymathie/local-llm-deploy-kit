# Remote Work Policy

This policy applies to all non-branch employees.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period may request to work remotely up to three days per week. Requests are approved by the employee's manager and recorded by Human Resources.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours, 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern time, Monday through Friday.

## Equipment and stipend

The credit union provides a laptop and a monitor. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair, or headset. Receipts for the stipend must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Security requirements

Credit union data may only be accessed on devices the credit union manages. A VPN connection is required on any network that is not your home network, including hotels and coffee shops. Printed documents containing member information must be shredded, not placed in household recycling. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk as the security incident reporting standard requires.
