# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all employees who travel for work.

## Approval

Trips that require air travel must be approved by a manager at least 14 days before departure. Trips within driving distance need no advance approval.

## Lodging and meals

Hotel stays are capped at $180 per night in most cities and $260 per night in high-cost cities. Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day while traveling. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the published standard mileage rate. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports with itemized receipts within 30 days of returning from the trip. Expenses submitted after 90 days require written approval from the finance team.
