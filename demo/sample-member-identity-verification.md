# Member Identity Verification (Cypress Harbor Credit Union, fictional sample)

This is a fictional sample standard for demonstration purposes only.

## Opening an account

New members provide one government-issued photo ID and one secondary document, such as a utility statement dated within 60 days.

## Phone and digital channels

Before discussing an account over the phone, verify the caller with a one-time code sent to the mobile number on file. Caller ID alone never proves identity. Staff must never ask a member to read back a full card number or online banking password.

## Contact changes

Changes to a member's phone, email or mailing address trigger a confirmation letter to the old address and a 7-day hold on new payees and wires.
