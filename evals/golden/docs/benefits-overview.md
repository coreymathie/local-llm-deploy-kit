# Benefits Overview (fictional sample)

This fictional overview exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Retirement

The company matches retirement plan contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of salary. The match vests immediately.

## Health coverage

Health, dental and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month after the start date. Dependents can be added during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

## Wellness and support

A wellness reimbursement of $600 per year covers gym memberships, fitness classes and sports equipment. The employee assistance program offers free, confidential counseling sessions.
