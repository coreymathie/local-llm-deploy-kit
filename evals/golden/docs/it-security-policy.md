# IT Security Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Password managers are approved and encouraged. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and every administrative console. Hardware security keys are issued to administrators.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may read email only through the managed mail app. Software from outside the approved catalog needs a security review before installation.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the service desk within 1 hour of noticing it is missing. The service desk wipes the device remotely and revokes its certificates.

## Phishing

Use the Report Phishing button in the mail client for suspicious messages. Never forward the suspicious message to colleagues. Security runs a phishing simulation every quarter.
