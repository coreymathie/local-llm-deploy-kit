# Remote Work Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Schedule

Remote employees keep core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Team meetings are scheduled inside core hours.

## Equipment and stipends

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $400. Coworking space memberships are reimbursed up to $150 per month.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager and HR approval. Longer stays need a review by the tax team.

## Security at home

Use the VPN on any network you do not control, such as cafes and hotels. Keep screens out of view of other people when working in public.
