# Business Travel Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Booking

Book all trips through the travel portal at least 21 days before departure when possible. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed on flights longer than 6 hours with written approval from a vice president.

## Lodging

Hotel rates are capped at $210 per night, or $290 per night in designated high-cost cities. Stays longer than 14 nights should use a serviced apartment.

## Ground transport

Rental cars must be compact or intermediate class. Rideshare and taxis are reimbursed between the airport and the hotel. Train travel is preferred for trips under 300 miles.

## Daily allowance

The per diem for meals and incidentals is $68 per day. The first and last travel days receive 75 percent of the per diem.
