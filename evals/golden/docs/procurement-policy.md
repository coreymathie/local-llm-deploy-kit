# Procurement Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Quotes and approvals

Purchases over $5,000 require three competitive quotes. Purchases over $50,000 require approval from the procurement committee, which meets every second Tuesday. Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is prohibited.

## New vendors

Every new vendor completes a security questionnaire and a tax form before the first purchase order. Vendors that will handle customer data also sign a data processing agreement.

## Payment

Standard payment terms are net 45 days from the invoice date. Early payment discounts may be accepted when finance approves.
