# Incident Response Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Severity levels

A SEV1 incident is a full outage or a confirmed data breach; the on-call responder must acknowledge it within 15 minutes. A SEV2 incident is a partial outage or degraded service; acknowledgement is required within 1 hour. A SEV3 incident is a minor bug with a workaround and is handled during business hours.

## Communication

During a SEV1, the incident commander posts a status page update every 30 minutes. Customer-facing messages are approved by the communications lead.

## After the incident

A blameless postmortem is written within 5 business days for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident. Action items from the postmortem are tracked to completion in the engineering backlog.
