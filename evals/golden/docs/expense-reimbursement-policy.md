# Expense Reimbursement Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports within 45 days of the expense. Itemized receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expenses older than 90 days are not reimbursed without a finance exception.

## Corporate cards

Corporate card statements must be reconciled by the 5th of each month. Personal purchases on a corporate card must be repaid within 10 days.

## Meals and gratuities

Client meals require the names of attendees and the business purpose. Tips are reimbursed up to 20 percent of the bill.
