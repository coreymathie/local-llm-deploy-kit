# Data Classification Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Levels

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Public data may be shared freely. Internal data is for employees only. Confidential data includes contracts and customer lists. Restricted data includes payment card data, health records and government identifiers.

## Handling restricted data

Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Restricted data may never be sent by email or stored on removable media. Access to restricted data is reviewed every 90 days.

## Labeling

Documents containing Confidential or Restricted data carry the classification label in the header. When unsure, classify one level higher.
