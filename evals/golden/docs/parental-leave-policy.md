# Parental Leave Policy (fictional sample)

This fictional policy exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Paid leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave applies to birth, adoption and foster placement.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible after 90 days of continuous employment. Leave must start within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Returning to work

A phased return is available for the first 4 weeks back, working 60 percent of normal hours at full pay. Lactation rooms are available in every office.
