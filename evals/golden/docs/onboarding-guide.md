# New Hire Onboarding Guide (fictional sample)

This fictional guide exists only to evaluate retrieval in this repository.

## Before day one

The laptop ships 5 business days before the start date. Accounts are created two days before the start date, and the sign-in link arrives in the personal email given during hiring.

## First weeks

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team. Managers share a 30-60-90 day plan during the first week. Required compliance training must be completed within 14 days of the start date.

## Getting help

The service desk is open from 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. The people team runs a new-hire question session every Thursday.
